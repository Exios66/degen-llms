# Casino simulator bug sweep — October 28, 2026

This sweep covered the Python CLI, browser terminal, and Phaser RPG. Parallel
subagents audited separate areas, followed by second passes and an independent
review of the combined changes. The starting checkout was clean. The original
282 Python tests and browser import checks passed before the audit; the defects
below needed additional coverage.

## Table games

- Corrected ace ordering in poker pairs, kickers, trips, quads, and flushes.
- Added Hold'em side pots, short all-in eligibility, uncalled excess returns,
  and deterministic odd-chip allocation.
- Fixed hands stalled by all-in blinds, out-of-turn actions, fractional browser
  raises, and betting incorrectly reopened by a short all-in raise.
- Started Hold'em hands with a complete deck and deferred blackjack cut-card
  reshuffles until the next round; a short shoe that runs dry mid-round now
  refills from cards not already in play instead of repeating one.
- Corrected blackjack soft totals with multiple aces, natural eligibility after
  splitting, total split limits, surrender eligibility, and double-after-split
  rules. CLI action prompts now reject full action words that are not legal for
  the current hand.
- Offered insurance before the dealer blackjack result, capped it by available
  bankroll, and prevented repeated browser actions and settlement.
- Returned earlier bot bets when a later human seat cancels CLI betting and
  validated standalone CLI configuration after applying flags.
- Fixed Don't Pass point progression and made browser craps wager validation
  atomic so a rejected line wager cannot consume side stakes; Ctrl+C at the CLI
  craps table now returns the working line, hardways, and unrolled side stakes.

## Markets, racing, and lottery

- Removed pushed parlay legs from payout odds; all-push parlays return the stake.
- Preserved prediction markets with open contracts across slate changes and
  advanced the prediction cursor after its initial page.
- Refunded legacy orphan prediction contracts and made their result screen
  tolerate a missing market.
- Settled linked sports predictions from the actual game, retained links across
  slate changes, and returned stakes on spread pushes or unavailable outcomes.
- Reconciled separately serialized copies of a sports event so tickets and
  predictions share one result after reload.
- Rejected new sports and prediction wagers after their outcome becomes known,
  before debiting the wallet.
- Fixed double-counted CLI sportsbook bet statistics.
- Expired options against the underlying quote instead of their premium;
  retained that spot on opened positions and supported older positions.
- Made reported futures losses equal actual chip movement after margin is
  exhausted, aligned fractional P&L truncation, and validated trade quantities.
- Kept open racing tickets attached to their original field, and prevented
  absent entrants from winning browser place/show bets.
- Rejected fractional/nonfinite lottery picks, handled nondecimal Unicode input
  safely in the CLI, and prevented empty browser picks from automatically winning.
  Duplicate Mega picks now count once, the first prediction slate loads the
  stored scenario database, and scratch-prize boundaries match the published
  weights.
- Settled CLI roulette spins and lottery tickets before their reveal animations,
  and finalized a pending browser lottery ticket when its host closes, so an
  interruption cannot void a determined win.

## Economy, saves, and resort lifecycle

- Rejected invalid/nonfinite wallet and bank amounts and negative reconciliation.
- Corrected reversed Delete/Exit choices in the CLI save picker; Ctrl+C at the
  picker exits cleanly, and `--new-save` no longer deletes the old slot before
  its replacement is written.
- Added atomic CLI save replacement, corrupt-index recovery, slot validation,
  browser backup-cache recovery, and fallback from a corrupt active profile;
  arcade drink-refill redemption updates the modeled rewards state instead of
  writing a web-shaped blob that could corrupt the next CLI load.
- Awarded intervening tier comps during rewards jumps and migrations.
- Corrected rollover day labels and stale overdue/eviction responses; evicted
  guests cannot regain keys without resolving their stay.
- Persisted checkout without room amenities, validated stay lengths, and limited
  one room-night voucher to one night; same-tier or lower "upgrades" no longer
  charge chips or consume a suite comp, and housekeeping tips appear once on the
  folio.
- Validated pending refunds and recorded them as refunds rather than wins;
  persisted bar visits/drink history across reloads and made positive phone-call
  and SMS chip/buffet gifts one-per-choice instead of repeatable faucets.
- Added interruption-safe CLI cleanup for unsettled market and racing stakes,
  retaining settled results without refunding already-paid principal again.
- Cashed out the live Hold'em table stack when the RPG host exits, the save is
  switched, or the page reloads, and confirmed mid-hand exits so committed chips
  are not forfeited by a stray click; Ctrl+C at the CLI table now returns the
  remaining stack.
